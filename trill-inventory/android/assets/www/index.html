Scan it. Count it.
Know what to order.

Trill Inventory is your barcode-driven stock counter. Point your phone at a product, and the app pulls that item's shelf price and details, tells you how many are left, and recommends what to reorder.

🔒 Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app starts empty — you build the inventory as you scan.

What's inside

  • 1
    Live barcode scanningCamera reads UPC, EAN, Code 128 and more.
  • 2
    Deep product detailsPrice, cost, margin, supplier, shelf, pack size.
  • 3
    Stock counts & reorder hintsSee units remaining and what to bring in.

How to use it

Four steps, every single time you scan something.

  • 1
    ScanTap Start camera and hold the barcode inside the frame, about 6–10 inches away. The app beeps and the flashlight flashes the moment it locks on.
  • 2
    ReviewA sheet slides up with the product's full details — price, cost, margin, supplier, shelf location, and how many you have left.
  • 3
    Catalog it, or don'tTap Catalog it to add it to inventory. Use − / + or the quick chips to set how many you're adding. Tap Don't catalog to just look it up.
  • 4
    Keep countingScan one at a time and each scan adds 1 to the count, or hit Add more to enter a whole case at once. The restock list updates itself.
💡 Unknown barcode? The app asks you to name it and set a price — it's saved to your catalog from then on.

Getting around

Two swipe gestures open everything. No hunting through tabs.

👉
Swipe right from the left edgeMain menu — inventory, restock list, history, price catalog.
👈
Swipe left from the right edgeOptions — scanning, inventory, appearance, alerts, data, advanced.
☰
Or just tap the buttonsSwipe not your thing? The ☰ and ⚙ icons do the same job.
🔊 Turn your volume up — the confirmation beep and flashlight flash are your signal that a scan registered.

You're set up

Inventory is empty. Let's put the first product in it.

  • 1
    Allow camera accessYour browser will ask once. If it's blocked or there's no camera, use Manual entry.
  • 2
    Scan your first itemPoint it at any product barcode — prices resolve from the online lookup.
  • 3
    Set the countTell the app how many are on the shelf and you're live.
⚡ Tip: the sheet stays open so you can adjust the count — tap Scan next when you're done with an item.
